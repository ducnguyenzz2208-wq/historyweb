# Lam Son Uprising: Ten years of tasting honey and lying on thorns

In early 1418, a tycoon of Lam Son (Thanh Hoa) named Le Loi raised an uprising flag against the Ming Dynasty's domination. In the early years, that small force was besieged many times to the point that they had to retreat to Chi Linh mountain, sometimes running out of food and having to kill elephants and horses. Yet ten years later, that same army forced 100,000 Ming troops to hold a meeting and swear to withdraw from Dai Viet. The Lam Son Uprising was a resistance war to regain independence after twenty years of fourth Northern domination, and opened the early Le Dynasty, which lasted nearly a century.[^1]

## From Lam Son to Nghe An: change direction to survive

After the defeat of the Ho Dynasty in 1407, the Ming Dynasty annexed Dai Viet into Giao Chi district, implementing a strict assimilation policy: forcing people to follow Northern customs, confiscating books, and raiding resources. Many uprisings broke out and were quelled.

The first three years of the Lam Son uprising also struggled similarly, struggling in the mountains and forests of Thanh Hoa and constantly being chased. The turning point came from a strategy of Nguyen Chich: abandoning the passive defense position in his homeland, sending troops into Nghe An - a large land, crowded with people, far from the enemy's power center - using it as a springboard.[^2] From 1424, the insurgents switched to an offensive position, successively mastering Nghe An, Tan Binh, and Thuan Hoa, then besieging citadels in the Northern Delta.

## Tot Dong – Chuc Dong and Chi Lang – Xuong Giang

The strategic mind next to Le Loi is Nguyen Trai, who composes letters to persuade people to surrender and outlines the way to "hit people's hearts". Two major campaigns decided the situation:

* **Tot Dong - Chuc Dong (late 1426):** The insurgents ambushed, defeated Vuong Thong's reinforcements, destroyed and captured tens of thousands of Ming soldiers near Dong Quan (Thang Long).
* **Chi Lang - Xuong Giang (late 1427):** When the Ming Dynasty sent two large reinforcements to rescue, the insurgents advocated "siege the city and destroy the reinforcements". Lieu Thang's army was stopped and attacked at the Chi Lang pass, the main general died in battle; The remaining members of Moc Thanh also fled when they heard the news. Vuong Thong in Dong Quan city lost all hope.

Instead of destroying it to the end, Le Loi and Nguyen Trai accepted the Ming army to make peace. At the end of 1427, the two sides held the Dongguan Oath Conference; The Ming army was provided with provisions, ships and horses to safely return home - a way to end the war to maintain long-term peace with the North.[^3]

## The great report of Binh Ngo and the early Le dynasty

In early 1428, Nguyen Trai, under orders from Le Loi, wrote *Binh Ngo Dai Cao* - a declaration of independence summarizing the resistance war, opening by affirming that Dai Viet was a civilized country with its own independence. Le Loi ascended the throne and founded the Le Dynasty, known in history as Le Thai To.

The early Le Dynasty then carried out land reform (military land policy), established a set of laws and a regulated examination system, reaching its peak under Le Thanh Tong. In the long term, the Lam Son uprising not only restored independence but also laid the foundation for a centralized Confucian state model that existed for many centuries.[^4]
