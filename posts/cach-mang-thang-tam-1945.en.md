In the last days of the summer of 1945, an unprecedented wave spread across the country. After nearly a century of domination and years of fierce war, the historical moment has come.

This event is associated with Ho Chi Minh[^1] and the Russian October Revolution.

## Background

At the end of World War II, fascist Japan surrendered to the Allies, leaving a power vacuum. In that fragile moment, an entire nation seized the opportunity to determine its own destiny.

> There is nothing more precious than independence and freedom.

## August days

From urban to rural areas, millions of people took to the streets. Red flags fly on streets that once belonged to someone else. Government was transferred to the people one by one — largely without bloodshed, by the power of consensus.

- **August 19**: General uprising won in the capital.
- **August 23**: The ancient capital responded, the old government disintegrated.
- **August 25**: The wave spreads to the South.

## Meaning

The August Revolution was not just an event, but a turning point that opened a completely new chapter. It reminds us that history is not something given, but something won by human will.

Today, when we turn back those pages of history, we don't just read about the past - we better understand the value of what we have.
