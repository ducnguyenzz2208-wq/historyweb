# NVIDIA: From game graphics to the Heart of Artificial Intelligence

NVIDIA Corporation is an American multinational technology company, headquartered in Santa Clara, California. NVIDIA's history is a testament to the power of a steadfast long-term vision, technical innovation and the ability to grasp the greatest technology trends of the 21st century.

## 1. Starting from an idea (1993 - 1999)
NVIDIA was founded in April 1993 by three technologists: **Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky** and **Curtis Priem**. With an initial capital of only 40,000 USD, they determined that the future direction of computing would be based on the power of hardware-based graphics.
In the early stages, the company encountered many difficulties, even on the brink of bankruptcy with product NV1. However, the launch of the RIVA 128 chip line in 1997 saved the company's life. 
* In 1999, NVIDIA officially introduced the concept of **GPU (Graphics Processing Unit)** through the GeForce 256 product, forever changing the standards of the 3D gaming industry.

## 2. CUDA and Vision beyond its time (2006)
One of NVIDIA's most important historical turning points was the release of the parallel computing platform **CUDA** in 2006. CUDA allows scientists and researchers to use thousands of GPU computing cores to solve complex mathematical problems, instead of being limited to rendering graphics.
The huge investment in CUDA initially did not bring immediate profits, but it created a software ecosystem that no competitor could match, preparing fertile ground for the AI ​​explosion.

## 3. Origins of the Deep Learning Revolution (2012)
In 2012, an artificial neural network called **AlexNet** overwhelmingly won the ImageNet image recognition competition. The special thing is that AlexNet is trained using NVIDIA GPUs (GTX 580). Scientists realized that the parallel processing architecture of GPUs perfectly solves the huge amount of computation that deep learning requires. NVIDIA immediately changed direction, placing AI at the core strategic focus.

## 4. The Era of Generative AI and Monopoly (2020s)
The advent of large language models (LLMs) such as ChatGPT (OpenAI) in late 2022 requires the most powerful supercomputers. NVIDIA becomes the exclusive "pick and shovel" supplier in the "AI gold rush".
* IC systems such as **A100, H100** and NVIDIA's DGX supercomputer system have become the backbone of the global technology infrastructure, contested by all technology giants (Microsoft, Google, Meta, Amazon).
* Thanks to that, the company's revenue and profits grew at a galloping pace unprecedented in the history of the semiconductor industry.

## 5. Global position
From a company specializing in making graphics cards for gamers, NVIDIA has risen to the trillion-dollar capitalization mark, sometimes surpassing both Apple and Microsoft to become the most valuable company on the planet (2024). NVIDIA is now more than just a hardware company, but a computing empire shaping the future of human civilization through artificial intelligence, self-driving cars, robotics, and the digital twin (Omniverse).
