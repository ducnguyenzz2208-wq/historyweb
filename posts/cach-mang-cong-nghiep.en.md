In the mid-18th century, in the textile mills of England, a silent revolution began - a revolution that would forever change the face of humanity.

## The power of steam

The invention of the steam engine freed people from the limitations of human and animal power. For the first time, machines could operate tirelessly, day after day.

- Spinning machines help increase productivity dozens of times.
- Locomotives shorten the distance between cities.
- Factories spring up, changing both landscape and society.

## The price of progress

But every step forward has its price. Crowded industrial cities, harsh working conditions and a growing gap between rich and poor were the dark side of this period.

> The Industrial Revolution gave us unprecedented wealth, while also raising questions that we are still answering today.

## From the past to the present

The modern world — with its factories, cities, and technology — is a direct offspring of this revolution. To understand it is to understand the very era in which we live.
