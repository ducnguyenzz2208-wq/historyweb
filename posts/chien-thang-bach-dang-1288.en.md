# Bach Dang Victory 1288: When the tide became a weapon

On April 9, 1288, the Yuan army's warships led by O Ma Nhi withdrew along the Bach Dang River to the sea. The water is rising, the boat is going smoothly and fast. When the water began to recede, thousands of wooden poles with iron heads placed in the riverbed emerged. The entire navy of an empire that had just conquered nearly the entire Eurasian continent was stopped, burned and destroyed in one day. This was the third time, and the last time, the Yuan Mongol army entered Dai Viet.[^1]

## The third invasion and the empty battle

In 1287, Kublai Khan launched a third campaign, assigning Thoat Hoan to command the infantry and O Ma Nhi to command the navy, accompanied by a fleet of food ships escorted by Truong Van Ho - lessons learned from the previous two failures, when the Yuan army captured Thang Long but gradually died of hunger and disease.

The Tran Dynasty responded with a mature strategy: withdrawing from the capital, implementing "an empty garden", avoiding decisive battle when the enemy was still strong. The decisive blow was again aimed at logistics - Tran Khanh Du defeated the food fleet at Van Don at the end of 1287. Losing food and supplies, the expeditionary army captured an empty Thang Long and could not last long. In early 1288, Thoat Hoan decided to withdraw.[^2]

## Pile yards and tidal calculations

Tran Quoc Tuan chose Bach Dang River, the waterway gateway to the Gulf of Tonkin, the place that witnessed the victories of Ngo Quyen in 938 and Le Hoan in 981. The army and people drove stakes of ironwood and ironwood into the creeks, hidden under the water during high tide.

The strategy consists of three layers tightly coordinated in time:

1. **Lure:** The light boat challenges the battle and then feigns defeat, leading the Nguyen boat group into the exact section of the river where the stakes were placed while the water was still high.
2. **Block:** When the tide recedes, troops lie in ambush on both banks and large fleets of boats pour out, using fire rafts and fire attacks to attack the stagnant formation.
3. **Destroy:** enemy boat collides with piles, breaks and runs aground; The soldiers jumped ashore and fell into the waiting army wings.

O Ma Nhi and Phan Tiep were captured alive. *Complete History of Dai Viet* records that the water of Bach Dang river was blood red at that time.[^3] Thoat Hoan's army retreating through Lang Son road was also continuously pursued and suffered heavy losses.

## The meaning of a defensive victory

After 1288, the Yuan Dynasty no longer organized any further invasions of Dai Viet; The relationship between the two sides changed to nominal tribute, in exchange for de facto independence. To modern historians, the value of Bach Dang does not lie in the size of its force but in the way a small country thoroughly exploited terrain, hydrology and time to neutralize its overwhelming numerical advantage.[^4]

From the mid-20th century until now, many pile yards have been excavated in Yen Giang, Dong Van Muoi and recently Cao Quy (Hai Phong), providing material archaeological evidence for historical records.
