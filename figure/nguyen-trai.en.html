Nguyen Trai is the embodiment of the "literary and martial artist" model in Vietnamese history: both a strategist of an uprising and a cultural celebrity with long-term influence.

Lam Son's brain

Joining Le Loi's Lam Son insurgent army, he played the role of strategic advisor. The policy of "attacking people's hearts" — using justice and military fortune to break the enemy's will — helped the resistance war against the Ming army win in 1427 with fewer losses.

An ancient epic poem

After the victory, he wrote Binh Ngo Dai Cao, considered the nation's second declaration of independence, affirming the civilization and sovereignty of Dai Viet.

Tragedy and recovery

In 1442, he was involved in the Le Chi Vien case and was convicted of extortion. More than twenty years later, King Le Thanh Tong exonerated him.

Analysis

Nguyen Trai's life shows a paradox of medieval power: the person with the greatest merit can also become a victim of the very court he serves. But his philanthropic thoughts and writings overcame personal tragedy to become a common legacy. In 1980, UNESCO honored him as a World Cultural Celebrity.
