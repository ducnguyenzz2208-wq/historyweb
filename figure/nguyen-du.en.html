Nguyen Du (1765–1820) is a great poet of the Vietnamese people, author of The Tale of Kieu - a masterpiece of Nom poetry considered the pinnacle of classical Vietnamese literature. In 2013, he was honored by UNESCO as a World Cultural Celebrity.

A lifetime between two dynasties

Nguyen Du was born into a noble family in Ha Tinh, his father and brother were both high-ranking mandarins under the Le - Trinh dynasties. But he grew up during the most chaotic period in history: the collapse of the Le - Trinh government, the Tay Son movement, and then the Nguyen dynasty took over. Those upheavals pushed him through many years of wandering, poverty, and witnessing "mulberry fields" - experiences that later permeated his works.1

The Tale of Kieu

Nguyen Du's masterpiece is Doan Truong Tan Thanh, commonly known as The Tale of Kieu - a poetic story with 3,254 six-eight verses, based on a Chinese novel but completely recreated in Vietnamese language and soul. Through the fate of the talented Kieu who endured fifteen years of wandering, the work is both a cry for the human condition - especially women - and a protest against an unjust society.2

Vietnamese master

He served as a mandarin under the Nguyen Dynasty and was an ambassador to China, but his name lives on thanks to his literature. In addition to The Tale of Kieu, Nguyen Du also left behind many collections of poems in Chinese and Nom such as Orientations of Ten Kinds of Sentient Beings. He died in 1820 in Hue during an epidemic.3

Analysis

Nguyen Du's position in Vietnamese culture is somewhat similar to Shakespeare's in English or Pushkin's in Russian: he not only wrote a masterpiece, but also raised the national language itself to a new level. The Tale of Kieu proves that Nom and Vietnamese are completely capable of conveying the most sophisticated levels of emotions and thoughts, at a time when Chinese still holds the official status. The vitality of the work is shown in the fact that it permeates folk life - Vietnamese people tell Kieu fortune-telling, Kieu retrieval, Kieu collection - turning a scholarly piece of literature into the common property of all classes. The humanitarian values ​​and voice defending people in his poetry still have the power to move to this day.
