Ly Thuong Kiet (1019–1105) was a great general of the Ly Dynasty, who commanded the resistance war against the Song army in the 11th century and was associated with the poem Nam Quoc Son Ha - considered the nation's first declaration of independence.

The eunuch leads the army

Originally named Ngo, real name Ngo Tuan, he was given the national name Ly thanks to his merits and became one of the pillars of the court under Ly Thanh Tong and Ly Nhan Tong. Although he was a eunuch in the palace, he rose through the ranks with true military and political talent, holding the highest positions in the state apparatus.1

"Immortal invention"

Faced with the threat of the Song Dynasty preparing to invade, Ly Thuong Kiet advocated a bold strategy: "sitting still and waiting for the enemy is better than sending troops forward to block the enemy's spearhead." In 1075, he led his army to attack Song land, defeating the provinces of Ung, Kham, and Liem - destroying the food warehouses and logistics bases that the Song army planned to use for the southern expedition, then proactively withdrew.2

Nhu Nguyet river defense line

When the Song army invaded in 1077, Ly Thuong Kiet established a solid defense line on Nhu Nguyet River (Cau River) to block the enemy. Here, legend has it that he had people recite the poem Nam Quoc Son Ha - affirming that "the mountains and rivers of the South dwell in the South" - to encourage the soldiers and dampen the enemy's spirit. The Song army was stopped, suffered heavy losses, and finally had to accept peace and retreat.3

Analysis

What makes Ly Thuong Kiet stand out in Vietnamese military history is his strategic thinking that combines both pre-emptive attack and active defense, along with the use of the spiritual element as a weapon. The raid on the Song Dynasty in 1075 was one of the earliest and rare examples of "distance defense" in history - actively disrupting enemy preparations right on enemy territory. As for Nam Quoc Son Ha, although researchers are still cautious about the true author, it has become a symbol of the sense of national sovereignty that was raised very early. He is the representative face of the spirit of the Ly Dynasty, the period that opened with the Edict to Move the Capital and the birth of Thang Long.
