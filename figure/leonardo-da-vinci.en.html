Leonardo da Vinci is often mentioned as the most typical "Renaissance man" - an intellect that refused to be limited in any field.

The artist

With only a few remaining paintings, he reshaped European painting. Mona Lisa and The Last Supper demonstrate the technique of sfumato — gradations so soft that boundaries seem to disappear — and an understanding of human psychology rare for their time.

Nature observer

His notebooks, written in reverse right-to-left script, contain thousands of drawings: anatomy of the body, the flow of water, the mechanics of bird flight, machines centuries ahead of their time.

Analysis

What's special about Leonardo is not that he was good at many things, but the way he saw: for him, art and science were two ways to understand the same world. Many of his ideas were not published during his lifetime and had little direct influence on contemporary technology. But it was his relentless spirit of curiosity — daring to question everything — that made him an immortal symbol of human intelligence.
