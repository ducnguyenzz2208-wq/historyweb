#Russia's October Revolution: The event that shook the world

The October Revolution (October according to Russia's old Julian calendar, corresponding to November according to the modern Gregorian calendar) in 1917 was one of the greatest turning points of the 20th century. Under the leadership of V. I. Lenin and the Bolshevik Party, the working class and working people of Russia rose up to overthrow the bourgeois government, establishing the first worker-peasant state in human history.

1. Historical Context: Russia before the storm

  • Situation of "Two parallel governments": After the February Revolution (1917) overthrew the absolute monarchy of Tsar Nicholas II, the political situation in Russia fell into a unique situation. On one side was the Provisional Government led by the bourgeoisie, on the other side were the Soviet (Council) of representatives of workers and soldiers.
  • Serious crisis: The Provisional Government decided to continue participating in World War I, causing the Russian economy to become exhausted, famine raging and the anti-war movement among the masses, especially farmers and soldiers, reaching its peak.
  • "April Theses": In April 1917, V. I. Lenin returned to Petrograd from abroad. He presented the April Theses, outlining the path to transition from the bourgeois democratic revolution to the socialist revolution, with the famous slogans: "No support for the Provisional Government!" and "The entire government goes to the Soviets!".

2. Main developments of the Armed Uprising

By the fall of 1917, the crisis had reached its peak. The Bolshevik Party, under the direct direction of Lenin, actively prepared for an armed uprising to regain power.

  • Night of October 24 (November 6, 1917): Lenin went directly to the Smolny Institute (Bolshevik headquarters) to command the uprising. Red Guard units, marines and revolutionary soldiers quickly occupied vital positions in the capital Petrograd such as railway stations, bridges, armories, telephone centers and post offices.
  • October 25 (November 7, 1917): Rebel forces surrounded the Winter Palace - the last stronghold of the bourgeois Provisional Government.
  • Night of October 25, early morning of October 26: The battleship Rang Dong fired a series of cannons signaling the attack order. The Winter Palace fell, and the ministers of the Provisional Government were arrested. The uprising in Petrograd won completely, without much bloodshed.

3. The Soviet government was established

On the night the Winter Palace was captured, the Second All-Russia Congress of Soviets opened, officially declaring the establishment of the Soviet government. The Congress passed two historic decrees drafted by Lenin:

  1. Peace Decree: Withdrawed Russia from World War I without the conditions of territorial division or reparations.
  2. Land Decree: Eliminate land ownership by landlords, nationalize land and redistribute it to poor farmers.

A new government called the Council of People's Commissars was established, with V. I. Lenin as President.

4. Great Historical Significance

  • For Russia: For the first time in history, workers (workers, farmers) stood up to take control of the country's destiny. Bringing Russia out of the imperialist war and paving the way for the construction of socialism, leading to the establishment of the Soviet Union (1922).
  • For the World: The October Revolution broke the largest segment of the capitalism system, opening a new era in human history - the era of transition from capitalism to socialism on a global scale. This event strongly encouraged the national liberation movement in colonial and dependent countries, including Vietnam.
