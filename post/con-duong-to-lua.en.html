Stretching thousands of kilometers across deserts, steppes and mountains, the Silk Road was one of the greatest trade networks ever created by humanity.

Not just silk

Although it was called silk — a precious commodity of the East — this route also transported countless other things: spices, precious stones, paper, gunpowder and even tea.

But the most important thing exchanged is invisible: thoughts. Religion, science, art and language followed caravans across the continent.

Legendary cities

Along the road, prosperous cities arise:

  1. Samarkand — the pearl of Central Asia.
  2. Dunhuang — gateway connecting China to the West.
  3. Constantinople — where the East meets the West.
Each camel caravan not only carries goods, but also carries an entire civilization.

Heritage

The Silk Road reminds us that the world was interconnected long before the internet age. The cultural fusion it created still resonates today.
