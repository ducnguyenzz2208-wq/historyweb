Ngo Quyen (897–944) was the king who started Vietnam's long period of independence and autonomy. With the Bach Dang victory in 938, defeating the Southern Han army, he ended more than a thousand years of Northern domination and was revered in history books as the "king of kings" who laid the foundation for the Dai Viet national system.

## Son-in-law of the Duong family

Ngo Quyen was from Duong Lam (now Hanoi), and came from a wealthy family. He is the minister and son-in-law of Duong Dinh Nghe - the person who holds autonomy in Vietnam after the Khuc family. When Duong Dinh Nghe was killed by general Kieu Cong Tien in 937, Ngo Quyen pulled troops from Ai Chau (Thanh Hoa) to interrogate him, forcing the traitor to ask for foreign help.[^1]

## Battle of Bach Dang and the stakes

Kieu Cong Tien asked for help from the Southern Han dynasty, opening the way for the army led by Liu Hoang Thao to enter. Ngo Quyen finished destroying the internal rebellion and then set up an underground stake formation on the Bach Dang estuary, taking advantage of the law of the tide: luring the enemy's boats at high water, then counterattacking when the tide receded, causing the boat to get entangled in the stakes and sink. Liu Hongcao died in battle, the invasion failed completely.[^2]

## The king who built the nation's foundation

In 939, Ngo Quyen proclaimed himself king, gave up the position of Northern Governor, established the capital in Co Loa and established his own court and officials - steps that affirmed that this was an independent country and no longer a district. He was on the throne for six years and then passed away; Then the country went through a rebellion of twelve warlords before being unified by Dinh Bo Linh.[^3]

## Analysis

Tran Dynasty historian Le Van Huu assessed that Ngo Quyen "could restore national unity" for the Vietnamese people - an assessment that condensed his historical role. The notable point is that his victory not only had temporary military significance, but created a psychological and political precedent: from 938, independence became the default state that every subsequent dynasty was determined to protect. His Bach Dang pile dumping plan was also re-applied by Le Hoan and Tran Hung Dao, turning this river into a recurring military symbol in the nation's history. The feat that made him famous was told in the Bach Dang Victory in 938, and repeated three centuries later in the Bach Dang Victory in 1288.
