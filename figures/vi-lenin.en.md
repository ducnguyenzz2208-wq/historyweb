**LEADER · REVOLUTIONARY**

#V.I. Lenin

**1870 — 1924**

On the night of October 25, 1917 (Julian calendar), in Petrograd, the Bolsheviks under Lenin's direction occupied the Winter Palace, overthrew the Provisional Government, and gave birth to the world's first Soviet state. That moment was the culmination of more than two decades of secret revolutionary activity, exile, and endless theoretical debate.

![V. I. Lenin in 1920](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Lenin%20in%201920.jpg?width=800 "V. I. Lenin, 1920. Official portrait — Wikimedia Commons (Public domain). |left")

## Journey to find your way

Born in 1870 in Simbirsk to a middle-class family, Vladimir Ilyich Ulyanov — later using the alias Lenin — entered the revolutionary path after his older brother Aleksandr was executed for plotting to assassinate the Tsar in 1887. Expelled from university for participating in student demonstrations, he taught himself law and Marxism, then was exiled to Siberia. His years in exile in Europe — Munich, London, Geneva, Zürich — shaped his core ideology: the need for a professional, strictly disciplined revolutionary party capable of leading the working class to overthrow the Tsarist regime.

## The creator

Returning to Petrograd in April 1917 after the February Revolution, Lenin announced the April Theses, calling for the transfer of all power to the Soviets of workers and peasants. He founded and led the Bolshevik Party, turning a small group of revolutionaries into a force capable of taking over the leadership of Russia. After seizing power, he signed the Brest-Litovsk peace treaty withdrawing Russia from World War I, nationalized land and industry, and led the Red side in the Russian Civil War (1918–1921) against the White forces.

## Analysis

Lenin's legacy is laced with both achievement and controversy. He was the first person to realize Marx's theory into a concrete state, laying the foundation for the Soviet Union and inspiring communist movements around the world, including Vietnam. But his period in power also saw harsh War Communism, the suppression of opposition parties, and the birth of the Cheka — the secret police agency that was the forerunner of the KGB. He died in 1924 after multiple strokes, leaving behind a power struggle within the party that led to the rise of Stalin. Up to now, the perception of Lenin is still deeply divided between different political and academic views.
