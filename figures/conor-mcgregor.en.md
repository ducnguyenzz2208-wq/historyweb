# Conor McGregor: Icon of Mixed Martial Arts and Sports Entertainment

Conor Anthony McGregor (born July 14, 1988) is an Irish MMA fighter and former two-weight champion of the Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC). He is recognized as the superstar bringing in the largest Pay-Per-View (PPV - pay television) revenue in combat sports history.

## 1. Poor Childhood and Early Career
Born in Crumlin, a suburb of Dublin (Ireland), McGregor grew up in difficult circumstances. He used to work as a plumber and lived on unemployment benefits. Discovering his passion for martial arts, he began training in boxing and then switched to mixed martial arts under coach John Kavanagh.
His MMA career began in small tournaments in Europe, where he quickly demonstrated the power of extremely damaging left-hand punches and won two championship belts of the Cage Warriors tournament.

## 2. UFC Domination and Historical Records
Joining the UFC in 2013, McGregor immediately created a storm thanks to his excellent fighting skills and public speaking talent, attracting all the media's attention.
* **Historic 13-second victory (2015):** At UFC 194, McGregor knocked out Jose Aldo - who was undefeated for 10 years - in just 13 seconds to win the Featherweight championship. This is the fastest title match to end in UFC history.
* **Two-weight champion (Champ-Champ - 2016):** At UFC 205, McGregor defeated Eddie Alvarez to win the Lightweight title, thereby becoming the first fighter in UFC history to hold championship belts in two different weight classes at the same time.

## 3. Billion dollar match with Floyd Mayweather
In August 2017, McGregor entered professional boxing to challenge undefeated legend Floyd Mayweather Jr. Despite losing by TKO in the 10th round, this match became a huge entertainment event, bringing McGregor an estimated salary of more than 100 million USD and raising his name beyond the boundaries of MMA.

## 4. Become a successful Entrepreneur
More than just a fighter, McGregor demonstrates excellent business acumen. In 2018, he launched the whiskey brand **Proper No. Twelve**. This brand quickly achieved huge revenue and then sold the majority shares for a value of about 600 million USD. He is regularly ranked among the world's highest-paid athletes by Forbes magazine.

## 5. Controversial but great legacy
McGregor's career was later affected by serious injuries (such as a broken leg at UFC 264) and some private scandals. However, it is undeniable that Conor McGregor is the person who single-handedly brought the sport of MMA from a niche market to a sport that attracts billions of audiences globally.
